I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Warsaw (MIMUW), advised by [Dr. Kunal Dutta](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/~kdutta/).
I work on processing high-dimensional data using **dimensionality reduction** and **sample compression**, with tools from **high-dimensional probability** and **computational topology**.

Previously, I completed a Master of Mathematics at the [Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore](https://www.isibang.ac.in/), where I worked with [Dr. Siddharth Pritam](https://siddharthpritam.com/) (CMI) on the S-homotopy of graphs, and a B.Sc. in Mathematics at Ramakrishna Mission Vidyamandira.
